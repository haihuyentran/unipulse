Key Takeaways

Across 20 tracked opportunities and issues (13,552 total interactions), conversations from international students cluster into 7 major themes. The two most urgent are Ease the Cost-of-Living Burden and Simplify the Visa Journey — both carry some of the highest conversation volume while running well below neutral sentiment.

On the positive side, Double Down on Student Support is Monash's strongest asset, with sentiment well above the average — a theme worth leading with in recruitment marketing rather than just fixing what's broken.
